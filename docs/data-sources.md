# Data Sources

This document tracks the current and planned data sources for the Sri Lanka IT Talent Intelligence platform.

## Official Benchmark Sources
- **Department of Census and Statistics — Sri Lanka**: Labour, education, and workforce statistics. (Historical/reference)
- **ICTA — National IT-BPM Workforce Survey**: Benchmark survey for workforce demand and supply. (Reference)

## Primary Live Sources
- **Greenhouse Job Board**: Primary connector for company ATS systems.
- **SmartRecruiters**: Secondary connector requiring ATS credentials.

## Restricted Sources (Do Not Scrape)
- **XpressJobs**: Do not scrape. Potential future official API partnership.
- **ikman Jobs**: Do not scrape without explicit permission.

*All data sources must be integrated according to their Terms of Service.*
